The University Program is the formal track for Story B — a way for labs, chairs and students to work with the Gym on studying how LLMs acquire engineering capability. It serves two audiences:

## For labs & chairs

A partnership, not a sales pitch — we provide the infrastructure, you bring the science.

- **Access to the environment** — the training loop, validation gates and sign-off pipeline.
- **The benchmark** — tasks with ground truth for measuring capability across models, versioned and reproducible.
- **A published method** — so your work builds on a documented foundation; co-publication is a natural collaboration surface.
- **A real, non-toy domain** — mechanical design to study LLM capability in: not just language or code, but geometry that has to be *right*.

## For students

- **Free academic access** — the full open-core engine for your department: no billing, no trial expiry.
- **Thesis infrastructure** — the task-battery format and fidelity metrics are a ready-made evaluation harness for BSc/MSc/PhD work on generative CAD, prompt optimization or agent verification. We provide tasks and baseline numbers; you bring the science.
- **A line that looks good on a CV** — "I trained engineering agents in a closed loop with provenance" is a differentiating line for job applications at OEMs, Tier-1s and defence primes in 2027–2030. We market the tool to your career, not to your coursework.

## What we ask in return

Stated plainly, up front: **feedback** (issues and task batteries), **open-source contributions**, and — where a thesis uses the harness — **a citation**. That's the whole exchange. No paid student seats, no "academic pricing" theater: free is free.
